ABC University Admission Guide

Admission opens on July 15.
The admission fee is Rs. 500.
Hostel fee is Rs. 20,000 per semester.
Library timing is 9 AM to 8 PM.
Students must carry their ID card.

The academic year is divided into two semesters.
Classes for the first semester begin in August.
Classes for the second semester begin in January.

Hostel rooms are allotted on a first come first serve basis.
Mess fee is included in the hostel fee.
Students living outside the campus can apply for a day scholar pass.

The Computer Science department offers courses in
Machine Learning, Data Structures, Operating Systems, and Web Development.

Attendance of at least 75 percent is required to appear for the final exams.
Students with attendance below 75 percent must apply for condonation.

The examination fee is Rs. 300 per semester.
Revaluation of answer sheets can be requested within 10 days of result declaration.

The placement cell conducts training sessions every semester.
Companies visit the campus for placements between December and March.
